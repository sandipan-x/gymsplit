# GymSplit

Build your perfect workout split free. Pick days per week and your goal — get push/pull/legs, upper-lower plans with exercises, sets and reps.

## Features
- Days-per-week to full split generator (PPL, upper/lower, 5-day hybrid)
- Goal-based programming: muscle, strength or general fitness sets/reps
- Exercise picker per day with smart swaps plus scheduled rest days
